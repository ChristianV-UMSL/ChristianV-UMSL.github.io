Things I'm working on

Gameboy advance screen and battery upgrade

I added quality of life upgrades such as a rechargable battery and a backlit screen to breathe new life into an old gameboy advance I purchased. This project involved soldering wires between the backlit screen and the gameboy's motherboard in order to enable control over the screen brightness.

⚙

Learning about linux mint

I received an old Imac from 2012 and in an attempt to keep it out of the piles of e-waste, I decided to install linux mint os. The results were fairly pleasant, being capable of runnning older video games and only stuttering when several tabs are open, something I believe a ram upgrade could remedy. Something like this is perfectly usable for basic web browsing and productivity. I believe it is also capable of being used as a self hosted media server.

✦

Toshiba laptop restoration

I repaired and upgraded an old touch screen laptop for a relative after their previous laptop stopped getting supported for updates. The work done on the toshiba laptop included a screen replacement, a ram upgrade, and a battery replacement. After the hardware fixes were completed, I cloned the contents of the old hard drive to a SATA SSD to speed up start times.
